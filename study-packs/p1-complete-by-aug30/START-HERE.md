## Daily operating rule

- Learn from the relevant coursebook pages.
- Complete every independent question without notes.
- Add at least 12-20 marks of exam-style practice.
- Mark on the same day in a different colour.
- Record the first wrong step in the tracker.
- Any topic below 60% must be retaught before progressing.
