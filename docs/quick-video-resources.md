# Quick Video Resource Queue

This is the starting video bank for Cambridge International AS Mathematics 9709 (Pure Mathematics 1 and Mechanics) and AS Business 9609.

Videos are supplements, not study sessions by themselves. A video counts as completed only when Rohan finishes the linked follow-up questions or recall task.

## Daily video rule

- Cap video time at 35 minutes per day.
- Before watching, write one question the video should answer.
- Watch actively: pause before worked steps and attempt them first.
- After watching, close the video and write five points from memory.
- Finish with at least three relevant exam questions.
- Record the topic, minutes watched, questions attempted, score, and one mistake in the dashboard.

### AS Mathematics 9709 Mechanics

**Resource:** [9709 M1 – Momentum](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FQVUvV29aOI) by Amaths Online Lessons for CAIE A-level Maths 9709

This is an entry point to the channel's Cambridge 9709 Mechanics sequence, which covers kinematics, graphs, force diagrams, Newton's laws, friction, momentum, work, power, energy, and general motion.

Use the sequence in syllabus order where possible:

1. Kinematics of motion in a straight line
2. Forces and equilibrium
3. Dynamics
4. Energy, work and power
5. Momentum

**Follow-up:** Draw the situation and define the positive direction before doing three questions. For every error, label it as modelling, sign/direction, algebra, units, or interpretation.

## Quality filter

Keep a resource in the permanent bank only if:

- it matches the current Cambridge syllabus;
- the explanation is clear enough for Rohan to reproduce without the video;
- it leads directly to exam-question practice;
- Rohan rates it at least 4 out of 5 after using it twice; and
- it does not promote predicted-paper leaks or substitute passive watching for practice.
