# Rohan AS Study System: Research Brief and 45-Day Plan

Prepared on 16 August 2026 for the October/November 2026 Cambridge International exam series.

- Cambridge International AS & A Level Mathematics 9709

## 3. Mathematics 9709 research

### Assessment structure

For the AS route described here, candidates take Paper 1 and Paper 4 in the same series.

| Paper | Marks | Questions | AS weighting |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Paper 1: Pure Mathematics 1 | 75 | 10-12 structured | 60% |
| Paper 4: Mechanics | 50 | 6-8 structured | 40% |

Non-exact answers normally need three significant figures, or one decimal place for angles in degrees. A scientific calculator is allowed; graphical calculators and symbolic algebra/differentiation/integration are not. Necessary working must be shown, because unsupported calculator answers do not earn relevant marks.

Source: [Cambridge 9709 syllabus for 2026-2027](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697427-2026-2027-syllabus.pdf).

### Pure Mathematics 1 syllabus map

1. Quadratics
   - Completing the square, discriminant, equations and inequalities
   - Linear/quadratic simultaneous equations
   - Equations quadratic in a function of x
2. Functions
   - Domain, range, one-one functions, inverse and composition
   - Graph transformations and the inverse reflection in y = x
3. Coordinate geometry
   - Straight lines, distance and midpoint
   - Circles, tangents and intersections
4. Circular measure
   - Radians, arc length, sector area and mixed geometry
5. Trigonometry
   - Sine, cosine and tangent graphs
   - Exact values, identities and equations in stated intervals
6. Series
   - Binomial expansion
   - Arithmetic and geometric progressions, including sum to infinity
7. Differentiation
   - Gradients, tangents and normals
   - Stationary points, increasing/decreasing functions and rates of change
8. Integration
   - Indefinite/definite integration
   - Areas and volumes of revolution

### Mechanics syllabus map

1. Forces and equilibrium
   - Force diagrams, components, resultants, equilibrium and friction
2. Kinematics in a straight line
   - Distance/speed versus displacement/velocity/acceleration
   - Displacement-time and velocity-time graphs
   - Calculus and constant-acceleration formulae
3. Momentum
   - Linear momentum and direct impact, including bodies that coalesce
4. Newton's laws of motion
   - Connected particles, pulleys, towing systems and resisting forces
5. Energy, work and power
   - Work by a force, kinetic/potential energy, work-energy and P = Fv

### Examiner-reported risk patterns

The June 2024 public examiner report points to errors the application should explicitly classify:

- Unsupported calculator answers or incomplete substitution/working
- Basic algebra/index/sign errors and weak prerequisite trigonometry
- Misreading the requested quantity or units
- Incorrect or incomplete graph labels and transformations
- Missing both roots, failing to reject invalid roots, or using the wrong interval
- Confusing area with volume of revolution
- In Mechanics, omitting annotated force diagrams
- Wrong signs/directions in energy, resistance, momentum and resultant-force problems
- Treating acceleration down a slope as g
- Using constant-acceleration equations when acceleration is variable and calculus is required
- Rounding too early or failing to give a direction with a velocity/resultant

Source: [Cambridge 9709 June 2024 principal examiner report](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/567737-june-2024-examiner-report.pdf).

## 4. Business 9609 research

### Assessment structure

| Paper | Marks | Structure | AS weighting |
|---|---:|---|---:|
| Paper 1: Business Concepts 1 | 40 | Four 5-mark compulsory questions; one 20-mark essay chosen from two | 40% |
| Paper 2: Business Concepts 2 | 60 | Two compulsory 30-mark data-response questions, six parts each | 60% |

Both papers assess knowledge, application, analysis, and evaluation. Across AS Business, the approximate assessment objective weighting is AO1 30%, AO2 30%, AO3 20%, and AO4 20%. Paper 1 is slightly more knowledge-heavy; Paper 2 places relatively more weight on evaluation.

Source: [Cambridge 9609 syllabus for 2026-2028](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/697371-2026-2028-syllabus.pdf).

### AS syllabus map

1. Business and its environment
   - Enterprise, entrepreneurs/intrapreneurs and business plans
   - Sectors and ownership forms
   - Business size, small businesses and growth
   - Objectives, ethics/CSR and SMART targets
   - Stakeholders and stakeholder conflict
2. Human resource management
   - Workforce planning, recruitment/selection, redundancy/dismissal
   - Morale, welfare, diversity, training and workforce relations
   - Taylor, Mayo, Maslow, Herzberg, McClelland and Vroom
   - Financial/non-financial motivation and management functions
3. Marketing
   - Demand, supply, markets, B2B/B2C and product/market orientation
   - Mass/niche marketing, segmentation and CRM
   - Primary/secondary research, sampling and data interpretation
   - The 4Ps, product life cycle, Boston Matrix, pricing, promotion and place
4. Operations management
   - Transformation, productivity, sustainability and added value
   - Job, batch, flow and mass customisation
   - Inventory charts, buffer inventory, re-order level, JIT/JIC and supply chains
   - Capacity utilisation and outsourcing
5. Finance and accounting
   - Finance needs, working capital and sources of finance
   - Cash-flow forecasts
   - Fixed/variable/direct/indirect costs, full and contribution costing
   - Profit, contribution, break-even and margin of safety
   - Budgets and favourable/adverse variances

### Examiner-reported risk patterns

The June 2024 examiner report makes the main scoring issue very clear: many candidates know content but leave marks behind through weak application and evaluation.

- Answer the exact command word; do not spend paragraphs on a one-word "identify" response.
- Do not add generic definitions or introductions when the question asks for an impact, advantage, disadvantage or decision.
- Use the case data selectively and explicitly. Naming the company alone is not developed application.
- Build analysis as a causal chain with at least two meaningful links.
- Prefer one developed argument to several listed points.
- In 12-mark questions, evaluation is half the marks. A repeated summary at the end is not enough.
- Evaluate throughout, weigh relevant alternatives, state what the answer depends on, and finish with a supported contextual judgement.
- Show calculation stages and units so process marks remain available after an arithmetic slip.
- Frequent weak areas in the public report included outsourcing, industrial marketing, business-size measures, JIT versus JIC, supply-chain management, and context-specific use of working capital.

Source: [Cambridge 9609 June 2024 principal examiner report](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/Images/566845-june-2024-examiner-report.pdf).

## 5. Grade-A readiness targets

Thresholds change with paper difficulty and cannot be predicted or guaranteed. Recent official evidence gives a useful safety range:

- Mathematics 9709 components 12+42: grade a was 95/125 in November 2025 and 111/125 in March 2026.
- Business 9609 components 12+22: grade a was 78/100 in November 2025 and 81/100 in March 2026.

Sources: [November 2025 thresholds](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-advanced/cambridge-international-as-and-a-levels/grade-threshold-tables/november-2025/) and [March 2026 thresholds](https://www.cambridgeinternational.org/programmes-and-qualifications/cambridge-advanced/cambridge-international-as-and-a-levels/grade-threshold-tables/march-2026/).

The system should therefore use readiness buffers rather than last-session cut-offs:

| Metric | Final readiness gate |
|---|---:|
| Pure Mathematics 1 | Median at least 65/75 over the latest five valid timed papers; no paper below 62 |
| Mechanics | Median at least 45/50 over the latest five valid timed papers; no paper below 42 |
| Mathematics combined | At least 112/125 on representative paired papers |
| Business Paper 1 | At least 34/40 |
| Business Paper 2 | At least 51/60 |
| Business combined | Median at least 85/100 over the latest three paired sets |

These are operational targets, not promises about the final grade.

## 6. Learning design

The system should favor active retrieval, spaced review, feedback, worked-example/problem pairs, and deep explanatory questions. These choices are supported by the US Institute of Education Sciences practice guide, which recommends spacing, interleaving worked examples with problem solving, quizzes for retrieval, tests to identify learning needs, and deep explanatory questions.

Source: [IES - Organizing Instruction and Study to Improve Student Learning](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/wwc/PracticeGuide/1).

### Daily workload: 4 hours 30 minutes of focused study

Breaks are additional and should not count as focused time.

| Block | Focused time | Purpose |
|---|---:|---|
| Retrieval launch | 20 min | Formulae, definitions, error-log recall and yesterday's weak items |
| Pure Mathematics 1 | 85 min | Teaching/repair followed by exam-style questions |
| Business | 80 min | Content retrieval, calculations and one structured response |
| Mechanics | 65 min | Models, force diagrams and exam-style problems |
| Adaptive correction | 45 min | Marking, redo without notes, error classification and next review date |
| Daily close | 15 min | Confidence rating, plan completion and tomorrow preview |

On full-mock days, the mock replaces subject blocks but marking and correction remain compulsory. A paper is not "done" until every lost mark is classified and the student can redo the missed method unaided.

### Question allocation algorithm

Each generated daily pack should contain:

- 50% from current weak objectives
- 25% from objectives due for spaced retrieval
- 15% mixed exam-transfer questions
- 10% confidence-building mastered material

Difficulty should aim for a 70-85% success rate during learning. A topic below 60% should trigger a worked example plus a near-transfer problem. Two successful reviews at 80% or above should expand the review interval.

Every question record needs: syllabus objective, paper, skill, mark value, difficulty, estimated time, provenance, generation seed/template, expected answer, mark scheme, validation status, and teacher-approval status.

## 7. Adaptive 45-day plan

The detailed daily assignment must adapt to the diagnostic results. The phase structure can be fixed now.

### Days 1-3: baseline and setup

- Sit one timed Paper 1, one timed Mechanics paper, one timed Business Paper 1 and one timed Business Paper 2 across three days.
- Mark with official schemes and record every lost mark by objective and error type.
- Test formula recall, Business definitions and Business response structure separately.
- Establish baseline accuracy, time-per-mark, blank-rate and confidence calibration.
- Begin study immediately; product development must not delay the diagnostic.

### Days 4-17: repair and complete coverage

- Cover every syllabus objective once, weighted toward diagnostic weaknesses.
- Use worked-example/problem pairs for weak Math skills.
- Complete a daily mixed Math set and a daily Business command-word exercise.
- Write at least one Business 8-, 12- or 20-mark response each day on rotation.
- Run short cumulative tests every third day.
- Gate at Day 17: no unvisited objective; at least 70% accuracy on taught-topic tests.

### Days 18-30: interleaving and exam technique

- Shift from chapters to mixed, timed sets.
- Complete four to six timed paper sections per week plus two full papers.
- Use blind correction: redo missed questions before viewing the full solution.
- Business answers receive separate AO1/AO2/AO3/AO4 scores.
- Gate at Day 30: P1 at least 58/75, P4 at least 38/50, Business paired score at least 75/100, with no major topic below 65%.

### Days 31-40: full-paper cycle

- Complete approximately one full paper per day, rotating the four components.
- The second half of each day is reserved for marking, error repair and weak-topic drills.
- Repeat a parallel question within 24 hours for every method error.
- Gate at Day 40: P1 at least 62/75, P4 at least 42/50 and Business at least 80/100 on recent valid papers.

### Days 41-44: Pure 1 peak and taper

- Give Pure 1 first priority while maintaining short Business and Mechanics retrieval.
- Complete the final full Pure 1 paper early enough to correct it; do not sit a heavy mock the night before the exam.
- Use only error-log questions, formula recall, selected hard questions and exam routines on the final day.
- Protect sleep and normal meal timing.

### Exam day 45 and post-Pure-1 sprint

- 30 Sep: Pure Mathematics 1 exam; brief debrief only, with no answer-discussion spiral.
- 1-4 Oct: Business Paper 1 emphasis, while maintaining Paper 2 and Mechanics.
- 5 Oct: Business Paper 1 exam.
- 6-7 Oct: Business Paper 2 data-response and evaluation sprint.
- 8 Oct: Business Paper 2 exam.
- 9-12 Oct: Mechanics-only final cycle: force diagrams, connected particles, variable acceleration, energy and mixed timed papers.
- 13 Oct: Mechanics exam.

## 8. Product plan

### Student dashboard

- Today's mission with required minutes, questions and completion state
- Exam countdown and paper-specific readiness gates
- Focus timer and session check-in/check-out
- Daily question player with hints, answer entry and correction workflow
- Mastery map by syllabus objective
- Error-log review queue
- Mock-paper centre and score history
- Streak/adherence display that rewards completed corrections, not raw screen time

### Teacher dashboard

- Schedule/assignment editor
- AI question approval queue
- Business rubric marking and Math working review
- Alerts for missed sessions, falling scores, repeat errors and workload overload
- Paper-level and topic-level trend reports
- Material library with provenance and licence/access controls
- Notification templates and delivery logs

### Excel operations workbook

Excel should be a teacher-facing control centre and portable export, not the sole production database. Proposed sheets:

1. `Config`
2. `Exams`
3. `Syllabus Map`
4. `Daily Plan`
5. `Question Bank`
6. `Assignments`
7. `Attempts`
8. `Error Log`
9. `Mock Scores`
10. `Mastery`
11. `Dashboard`
12. `Notification Queue`
13. `Sources`

Raw inputs and assumptions must be separated from formula-driven outputs. Mastery, adherence, rolling paper scores, due reviews and readiness gates should be formulas that can be audited.

### Safe question generation

- Official past-paper questions stay labelled as official and retain source metadata.
- Generated questions are new variants mapped to syllabus objectives, not disguised copies.
- Math variants use constrained parameter templates and automatic numeric/symbolic checks before release.
- Business variants use short original scenarios and explicit AO rubrics.
- Initially, all generated 12- and 20-mark Business questions and mark schemes require teacher approval.
- The system must never invent a Cambridge attribution or claim an AI question is an official paper question.

### Notifications

- Morning: today's plan and first action
- Midday: reminder only if no study session has started
- Evening: incomplete-work prompt to the student and summary to the teacher
- Weekly: progress report, readiness gates and the next week's focus

Email can be sent through a transactional email adapter. WhatsApp should use the official Cloud API, not browser automation. WhatsApp policy requires the recipient's number and opt-in; business-initiated reminders require approved message templates, and free-form replies are limited to the 24-hour customer-service window. Credentials and phone numbers must never be committed to Git.

Sources: [WhatsApp Business Messaging Policy](https://whatsappbusiness.com/policy/) and [Meta WhatsApp developer overview](https://developers.facebook.com/documentation/business-messaging/whatsapp/overview).

## 9. Delivery sequence

The study system must be usable before it is polished.

1. Day 0-1: confirm entries and baseline; create the workbook schema and diagnostic sheets.
2. Day 1-2: produce the first seven days of assignments and a reviewed question pack.
3. Day 2-4: build the student dashboard, login, daily mission, attempt capture and teacher review.
4. Day 4-5: add email reminders and daily/weekly summaries.
5. After core stability: add WhatsApp onboarding, opt-in, approved templates and delivery receipts.
6. Ongoing: import authorised past papers and mark schemes, tag them, and generate validated variants.

The application build should never consume the brother's study window. The workbook and first assignments are enough to start while the dashboard is being built.

## 10. Information required to lock the schedule

- Statement-of-entry photo/PDF or confirmed component codes and administrative zone
- Current school/teacher grades and most recent mock scores
- Completed versus uncompleted topics in all four papers
- Daily availability, school hours, tuition and sleep schedule
- Calculator model and access to a printer/tablet
- Available textbooks, notes, past papers and mark schemes
- Student email; WhatsApp number only later through secure configuration
- Teacher email and preferred notification times
